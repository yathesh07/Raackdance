##### Branch Manager
[IMG images-raack/careers/evaPinFill2.svg alt="Dance classes in Mogappair"]

Details
Job Title: Branch Manager
A Branch Manager at a dance studio oversees day-to-day
                              operations, manages staff, schedules classes, and ensures customer satisfaction. You are
                              responsible for the smooth functioning of the branch, from financial management to
                              maintaining a safe and welcoming environment for students.
Requirements:
Operations Management: Oversee day-to-day operations of the dance studio branch. Ensure smooth functioning of classes, rehearsals, and events.
Staff Supervision: Recruit, train, and manage dance instructors and support staff. Provide guidance and support to staff members.
Scheduling and Coordination: Develop and maintain class schedules, ensuring optimal utilization of studio space and
                                 resources. Coordinate with instructors to ensure classes run smoothly and on schedule.
Customer Relations: Interact with parents, students, and clients to address inquiries, concerns, and
                                 feedback. Ensure a high level of customer satisfaction and resolve any issues promptly.
Financial Management: Monitor and manage the branch budget, including revenue, expenses, and profitability. Implement pricing strategies and promotions to meet revenue goals.
Marketing and Promotion: Work with the marketing team to develop and execute marketing strategies to attract new
                                 students and retain existing ones. Organize and promote events, workshops, and performances.
Facility Maintenance and Safety: Ensure the cleanliness, safety, and maintenance of the studio space. Implement safety protocols and emergency procedures.
Curriculum Development: Collaborate with dance instructors to develop and refine class curriculum. Ensure that classes align with the studio's overall teaching philosophy and standards.
Administrative Tasks: Handle administrative duties such as scheduling, payroll, and record-keeping. Maintain accurate and up-to-date student and staff records.
Reporting and Communication: Provide regular reports to the management team on branch performance, including
                                 financial metrics, enrollment numbers, and customer feedback. Communicate effectively with the head office and other branches.
Ready to make an impact through your managerial skills? Apply now with your resume and a
                              brief cover letter sharing your interest in joining Raack Dance Academy.
